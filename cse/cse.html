Six high-intensity competition modules engineered by the Department of Computer Science & Engineering.

01 TECHNICAL // PRESENTATION

PAPER PRESENTATION (TECHNICAL)

Abstract submission required before the deadline (250–300 words). Topics must focus on emerging technologies (AI, ML, Cybersecurity, IoT, Cloud).

👥 1–3 MEMBERS ⌖ M421 SMART TV CLASS ROOM ◷ 10:30 AM – 1:00 PM
02 HACKATHON // SPRINT

HACKATHON

Problem statements are pre-announced or released at the start. All code must be written during the event duration. Participants must bring their own hardware/laptops.

👥 2–4 MEMBERS ⌖ M422 SMART TV CLASS ROOM ◷ 10:30 AM – 1:00 PM
03 PROJECT // EXPO

PROJECT EXPO (MOBILE APP DEVELOPMENT)

Projects must be working mobile applications (Android/iOS/Flutter/React Native). Architecture diagram must be displayed.

👥 1–4 MEMBERS ⌖ GAMMA LAB ◷ 10:30 AM – 1:00 PM
04 AI // POSTER MAKING

SNAP WITH AI (POSTER MAKING)

Create high-impact visual posters using AI image generators (Midjourney, DALL-E, Leonardo AI, Canva AI). Prompts used must be logged and submitted.

👥 1–2 MEMBERS ⌖ EPSILON LAB ◷ 10:30 AM – 12:30 PM
05 TECH // QUIZ

CYBER HUNT (TECHNICAL QUIZ)

Round 1 (Prelims): Time-bound MCQs on Networking, OS, Cryptography and Security. Round 2 (Finals): Capture-The-Flag (CTF) challenges and log analysis.

👥 1–2 MEMBERS ⌖ EPSILON LAB ◷ 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM
06 PROMPT // BATTLE

AI PROMPT BATTLE (USING CLAUDE AI)

Identical target output requirements are provided to all contestants. Competitors write and refine prompts using Claude AI within the time limit. Single-user challenge.

👤 SINGLE PARTICIPANT ⌖ GAMMA LAB ◷ 2:00 PM – 3:30 PM
